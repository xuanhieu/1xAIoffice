The Start Center has VI and EN buttons. Click the language you want and 1xAIoffice restarts with every menu, button, dialog and quick start guide in that language.

## Steps {#steps}

1. **Open the Start Center** (the window you see when the app opens or when all documents are closed).
2. **Click VI or EN** at the top.
3. **Agree to the restart** if asked. If a document has unsaved changes, the app warns you first: when it reopens, a recovery step brings the document back.

## Autosave and recovery {#autosave}

1xAIoffice saves recovery information every 2 minutes, autosaves files that already have a name and keeps a backup copy on every save. If the app closes unexpectedly or restarts, the next launch offers recovery. Change these under Tools ▸ Options ▸ Load/Save ▸ General.

## What it does not do {#limits}

- Switching the interface does not change the spell-check language or your documents.
- DOCSO and DOCTIEN read numbers in Vietnamese in both interfaces.
