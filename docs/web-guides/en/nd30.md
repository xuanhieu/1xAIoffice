Click Decree 30 Document (or the Official letter, Proposal, Decision or Minutes card on the Start Center), fill in one form, and 1xAIoffice builds the document in the Decree 30/2020/NĐ-CP format: national title and motto, number and code, subject line, signature block and recipients.

## Where to open it

- **Start Center:** the Official letter, Proposal, Decision and Minutes cards (the form opens with that type selected) and All Decree 30 templates.
- **1xDoc:** the AI tab, the AI sidebar, or Vietnamese menu ▸ New Administrative Document (Decree 30).

## Steps {#steps}

1. **Pick the document type.** There are 28, from official letters (công văn), decisions (quyết định) and reports (báo cáo) to minutes (biên bản), proposals (tờ trình), contracts, invitations and leave forms.
2. **Fill in the form.** Required: issuing agency, agency abbreviation, place, subject and signer's full name. Optional: parent agency, number, date, addressee (letters), body (one paragraph per line), signing authority (TM., Q., KT., TL., TUQ.), position, a second signer, recipients (one per line), filing unit.
3. **Create.** If the current document is blank, the result fills it; otherwise a new window opens.

When you are done, run the [format check](/office/en/kiem-the-thuc) to verify margins, font and size before printing.

## What it does not do {#limits}

- Contract, memorandum, agreement and power-of-attorney templates only lay out the headings and labels, with no clauses. This is not legal advice.
- The app does not check the content or legal validity of the document.
- To have AI write the body, use the soan-van-ban-nd30 skill in [Run Skill](/office/en/skill).
- Documents are in Vietnamese, as the decree requires.
