Check Decree 30 reads the document open in 1xDoc and lists everything that does not follow the Decree 30/2020/NĐ-CP format. Paper size, margins and the default font can be fixed in one click, and one undo reverts it.

## What it checks

- **Paper:** A4.
- **Margins:** top and bottom 20–25 mm, left 30–35 mm, right 15–20 mm.
- **Font:** Times New Roman; body text 13–14 pt.
- **Paragraphs:** justified, first-line indent 10–12.7 mm, line spacing 100–150%, 6–12 pt after each paragraph.
- **Parts:** national title (Quốc hiệu), motto (Tiêu ngữ) and the "Nơi nhận:" recipients section are present.

## Steps {#steps}

1. **Open the document in 1xDoc** and click Check Decree 30 (AI tab, AI sidebar, right-click, or Vietnamese menu ▸ Check Decree 30 Format).
2. **Read the list of problems.**
3. **Accept the page fix** when the app offers it. It sets paper size, margins and default font; fix the other items in the text.

## What it does not do {#limits}

- It does not check wording, content or legal validity.
- It does not change font size or spacing or add the national title by itself; it tells you so you can fix them.
- 1xDoc only.
